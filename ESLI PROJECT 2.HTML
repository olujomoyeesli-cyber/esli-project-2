Mini Biography

Dr KWAME NKRUMAH was born on 21st September 1909 in Nkrumah, Ghana. He was a Ghanaian politician, revolutionary, and the first Prime Minister and President of Ghana. Nkrumah was a key figure in the African independence movement and played a significant role in the decolonization of Africa.

Nkrumah was educated in the United States and the United Kingdom, where he studied political science and philosophy. He returned to Ghana in 1947 and became involved in politics, advocating for independence from British colonial rule.

In 1957, Ghana became the first African country to gain independence, and Nkrumah became its first Prime Minister. He implemented various social and economic reforms, including the establishment of schools, hospitals, and infrastructure projects. Nkrumah also promoted Pan-Africanism and worked towards the unity of African nations.

However, Nkrumah's presidency faced challenges, including political opposition and economic difficulties. In 1966, he was overthrown in a military coup while on a state visit to North Vietnam. Nkrumah spent the rest of his life in exile, primarily in Guinea, where he continued to advocate for African unity and development.

Dr Kwame Nkrumah passed away on 27th April 1972 in Bucharest, Romania. He is remembered as a visionary leader who played a crucial role in the fight for African independence and the promotion of Pan-Africanism.

Legacy

Nkrumah's legacy continues to influence African politics andthe struggle for independence across the continent. He is celebrated for his contributions to Ghana's development and his efforts to promote African unity. His ideas and writings continue to inspire generations of Africans and leaders woworld wide.
